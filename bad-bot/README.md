# 🤖 Bad Bot Prompt Experiments

This folder contains initial prompt experiments that demonstrate **ineffective or poorly structured AI interactions**.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of this section is to:
- Understand how **bad prompts affect AI responses**
- Identify common mistakes in prompt design
- Build a baseline for improvement

---

## 📂 Contents

- `bad-bot-prompt.md`  
  Contains examples of poorly written prompts and their corresponding AI outputs.

---

## ⚠️ Observed Issues

From these experiments, several problems can be seen:

- ❌ Vague or unclear instructions  
- ❌ Lack of context  
- ❌ No structure or formatting  
- ❌ Ambiguous expectations  
- ❌ Generic or low-quality AI responses  

---

## 🧠 Key Learning

Bad prompts lead to:
- Inconsistent results  
- Low-quality answers  
- Misinterpretation by AI  

This highlights the importance of:
- Clear instructions  
- Specific context  
- Structured prompts
