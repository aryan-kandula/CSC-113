# AI For Everyone – Generative AI Micro-Course

## Course Overview
This repository documents my progress in **Module 1** of the  
**“Generative AI for Everyone”** micro-course by **Dr. Andrew Ng**, offered by DeepLearning.AI.

The course introduces foundational concepts in Generative AI, including:
- What Generative AI is and how it works
- Real-world use cases
- Capabilities and limitations of modern AI systems
- Responsible and practical adoption of AI tools

## Assignment Requirement
For this assignment, the requirement was to complete **Module 1** and submit a screenshot
showing progress. Quizzes are optional for free learners and were not required for credit.
