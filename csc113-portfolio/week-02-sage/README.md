# SAGE: My Study Assistant & Guide Engine

## What is SAGE?
SAGE is my personal AI-powered study assistant that helps me understand course material,
organize learning tasks, and improve how I study. I am building SAGE to support my
college courses while learning how to work effectively with AI tools.

## How I'll Use SAGE This Semester
- Explain difficult concepts in simple terms
- Help plan assignments and study schedules
- Brainstorm ideas and organize notes
- Review concepts before exams
- Reflect on my learning progress

## Tools I'm Using
- **Primary**: ChatGPT – Clear explanations and structured responses
- **Secondary**: Claude – Helpful for writing and alternative explanations
- **For Special Tasks**: Gemini Flash – Fast responses and experimentation

## Ground Rules for Working with AI
- Use AI as a learning aid, not a shortcut
- Always understand the output before submitting work
- Never submit AI-generated work without reviewing it
- Ask better questions when results are unclear

## Week 2 Learning Log – AI Collaboration (SAGE)

### Which AI tool did you prefer? Why?
I preferred **ChatGPT** because it is more conversational and adaptive. It helps me reason through problems step by step instead of just producing an answer, which makes it easier to understand *why* something works, not just *what* works.

### How did your prompts improve from first attempt to last?
My early prompts were vague and led to generic responses. Over time, I learned to add context, constraints, and clear goals. This resulted in more accurate, relevant, and actionable outputs with less revision needed.

### What surprised you about working with AI?
I was surprised by how much the quality of the response depended on how clearly I explained my intent. The AI felt less like a search engine and more like a collaborator that improves as communication improves.

### What are you still confused about?
I am still learning how to balance using AI for guidance versus solving problems independently. Knowing when to ask for help and when to work through a challenge myself is something I am actively improving.

### How will you use SAGE going forward?
I will use SAGE primarily as a planning, clarification, and reflection tool — helping me break down assignments, confirm my understanding, and improve documentation rather than using it as a shortcut for answers.
